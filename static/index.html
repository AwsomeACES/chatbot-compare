Scoring

LLM answers vary between runs; repeats give steadier scores and tighter ranges (and cost more).

Overall = 30% accuracy + 15% safety (prompt injection, guardrails, out-of-scope) + 15% no hallucination + 10% fairness (bias) + 20% judge quality + 10% speed. Speed is scored against fixed targets (median: 100 at 2s or less, 0 at 20s; p95: 100 at 5s, 0 at 40s), so adding a bot never changes another bot's score. Without the judge, quality is dropped and the rest rescaled.

A bot is ranked only when at least 80% of its cases were scored. Below that (e.g. most cases blocked by an API quota) its overall score is hidden and its row greyed out, so it can't look like a winner on a handful of cases.

Judge review

Read each conversation and record the right answer. The queue shows, in order: conversations where the two judges disagree, where the exact checks and the judge disagree, then a fixed random sample where nothing disagrees. After about 50 labels you know how far to trust the judge: the random spot-checks estimate its accuracy on every conversation, the disagreements show whom to believe when graders conflict.

Blind arena

Ratings are fitted to all votes at once (Bradley–Terry), so vote order doesn't matter, and a battle survives a page reload or server restart.
